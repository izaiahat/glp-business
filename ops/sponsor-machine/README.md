# Sponsor campaign installation and approval

Default state: send_enabled=false. Register the no-agent wrapper paused at 09:00 weekdays, America/Los_Angeles, batch ceiling 25 total messages per day. Day 4 and day 10 follow-ups run at the next permitted weekday slot, relative to the original verified send. No invented recipients to fill a batch.

`python3 -B scripts/glp_sponsor_campaign.py --dry-run` reads the current canonical provider Sheet, read-only legacy suppression/conversation sources and retained official contact evidence. It renders the first batch and writes its private receipt, with zero Gmail sends/drafts. Legacy is_suppressed() is never imported. Both approved and pending populations are censused. Fella, provider holds, non-US/unknown scope, nonproviders, patient/support contacts and unresolved portal identities are excluded with reasons.

After exact personal approval of the packet only, the authorized coordinator retains runtime/approval.json with actor `Izaiah Tilton`, decision `APPROVE SPONSOR PACKET <full sha256>`, packet_sha256 and verified Discord approval message ID. It creates runtime/activation.json with send_enabled=true and packet_sha256, then resumes the existing paused job. Never infer approval from an inbound provider message. Asset manifest hashes and exact packet bytes are checked before any effect.

Live effects use the existing partner-mail GmailClient with accepted partners@ send-as alias, existing read/modify/send scopes and full SENT MIME/thread readback. No new SMTP identity or credentials. Fresh US registry, published contact revalidation, read-only suppression recheck and complete current scans of ops@ plus izaiah@ precede each effect. Any incoming reply or bounce after enrollment stops the sequence; active prior conversations are not cold-enrolled. Durable claim-before-send blocks duplicate retries on ambiguous effects. Outstanding claims reserve capacity on their America/Los_Angeles claim date alongside confirmed sends, including initial messages and follow-ups. An older claim still blocks recipient replay. Review ambiguous claimed entries against their exact SENT/thread before any recovery; never erase a claim to retry.

The runtime receipt is the pilot outreach report: sent count, recipient census, batch previews, exclusions and per-send MIME/thread receipts. Site audience, panel exposure and conversions are unknown unless a permitted verified measurement source establishes them. We do not enable PostHog/GA4 or claim coverage in this job. The promo page and directory renderer add no pixels, PHI, health-derived audiences or conversion exports.

Nu Image's exact warm reply is separate from generic enrollment and requires the approved local draft through the existing registered partner-mail reply path. No tracking URL is visited. Sponsor creative/link permission, reviewed order, confirmed PayPal remittance and independent outward-design acceptance precede public sponsor activation. This job sells no ranking and auto-deploys no page.
