What is Mondae?

Mondae is a telehealth platform that connects eligible patients with licensed healthcare providers for evaluation and, when medically appropriate, prescription treatment. If prescribed, medications are dispensed and shipped by a licensed pharmacy, with ongoing access to your healthcare provider throughout your treatment.

How does Mondae work?

Begin by completing a secure online intake questionnaire detailing your health history, current medications, and treatment goals. A U.S.-licensed healthcare provider will review your information and conduct a medical consultation to determine whether treatment is appropriate for you. If prescribed, your medication will be shipped directly to your door with tracking information. Throughout your treatment, you can securely message your healthcare provider through your online patient portal with questions, dosage concerns, or to request follow-up consultations and treatment adjustments as medically appropriate.

Do I need insurance?

No, insurance is not required.

Will I get charged if I don’t qualify for treatment?

No, you won’t be charged until a physician approves your treatment.

What states is Mondae available in?

Mondae is available in all 50 states.

Can a provider change my dosage at any point during treatment?

Yes. Your provider can adjust your dosage if needed, depending on your medical history and how you respond to treatment.

Who will prescribe my medications?

Your medication is prescribed by state-licensed U.S. clinicians and dispensed by licensed U.S. pharmacies. Mondae connects you with providers for evaluation and ongoing care through your patient portal.
